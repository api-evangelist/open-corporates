OpenCorporates, the largest open database of legal entity data in the world, today announced that it has achieved certification to ISO/IEC 27701:2025, the international standard for privacy information management systems (PIMS). The certification was awarded following an independent audit by DNV, which recorded no non-conformities against the standard. It sits alongside the company's existing ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification for information security, making OpenCorporates a dual ISO certification holder for the first time in its history.
